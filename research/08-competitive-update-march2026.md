## Treatment Verification: The Killer Use Case

### The Problem
- Water utilities are installing $500K-5M PFAS treatment systems (GAC, IX, RO)
- Treatment costs: $0.03-28/m³ operational
- But they CAN'T VERIFY treatment is working in real-time
- Lab testing takes 2-4 weeks — utility is distributing water blindly
- If treatment breakthrough occurs, weeks of non-compliant water delivered

### TRACE-1 as Treatment Verification
- Monitor GAC bed influent AND effluent daily/hourly
- Detect breakthrough immediately (when PFAS in effluent exceeds limit)
- Optimize GAC replacement schedule (currently replaced on fixed schedule)
- Cost savings: Extending GAC bed life by 10% saves $50K-200K/year
- Risk reduction: Prevent non-compliant water distribution

### This Changes the Value Proposition
- Not just "$75 test vs $400 lab test"
- It's "prevent $100K+ in wasted GAC + regulatory violations"
- The TRACE-1 reader pays for itself in 1-2 months of GAC optimization

## FREDsense Update
- Still the only commercial PFAS field test (FRED-PFAS)
- 3-4 hour turnaround (too slow for treatment monitoring)
- Detects total PFAS (not speciated PFOA/PFOS)
- Biological reagent system (less stable than MIP)
- Positioned for site screening, not real-time monitoring

## MIT/Nature Water (March 2025)
- FET sensor for PFOS detection in tap water
- "Reversible parts-per-trillion-level detection"
- Uses field-effect transistor with functionalized surface
- Published in Nature Water — high credibility
- Academic prototype, no commercial path yet
- But validates the market need and technical approach
