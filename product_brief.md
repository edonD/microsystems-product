# Product Brief: VERIFLUX — Microfluidic PFAS Detection Platform

## Company
**Veriflux Microsystems** — Real-time water intelligence, powered by microsystems engineering.

## Product
**TRACE-1** — A handheld microfluidic electrochemical analyzer that detects PFAS contamination in drinking water in under 15 minutes, at a fraction of laboratory costs.

---

## The Problem

### Forever chemicals are everywhere, and we can't see them
Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) — "forever chemicals" — contaminate the drinking water of over 100 million Americans. In April 2024, the EPA set the first-ever legally enforceable maximum contaminant level (MCL) for PFOA and PFOS at **4 parts per trillion** — the equivalent of detecting 4 drops in 250 Olympic swimming pools.

### Testing is broken
The only way to test for PFAS today is to ship a water sample to a specialized laboratory equipped with a $500,000+ LC-MS/MS system. This means:
- **$250–600 per test**
- **2–4 weeks for results**
- **Only ~200 certified labs** in the US (massive backlog)
- **No real-time monitoring** — treatment plants can't verify their systems are working
- **Quarterly sampling** when daily/weekly is needed

### 66,000 water systems must comply by 2031
Every public water system in America faces mandatory PFAS monitoring. The total cost of compliance is estimated at **$1.5 billion per year** in testing alone. Water utilities, military bases, airports, industrial facilities, and environmental consultants all need faster, cheaper PFAS testing — yesterday.

## The Solution

### TRACE-1: Lab-quality PFAS detection in your hand
Veriflux TRACE-1 is a portable analyzer that uses a disposable microfluidic chip to detect PFOA and PFOS at sub-4 ppt levels in under 15 minutes, directly at the point of need.

### How it works
1. **Collect**: Draw a water sample into the disposable cartridge
2. **Insert**: Snap the cartridge into the handheld TRACE-1 reader
3. **Analyze**: Microfluidic channels deliver the sample across MIP-functionalized electrodes. Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy measures PFAS binding with sub-ppt sensitivity
4. **Result**: Quantitative PFOA and PFOS concentrations displayed in under 15 minutes. Data auto-uploaded to the Veriflux cloud dashboard

## Technology

### Core innovation: MIP-functionalized microfluidic electrochemical chip
The TRACE-1 cartridge is a MEMS-fabricated microfluidic chip containing:

1. **Molecularly Imprinted Polymer (MIP) electrodes** — Synthetic antibody mimics created by electropolymerizing conducting polymers around PFAS template molecules on gold interdigitated electrodes. After template removal, the polymer retains nanoscale cavities that selectively rebind target PFAS molecules.

2. **Microfluidic sample handling** — Serpentine channels precisely deliver sub-milliliter water samples across the sensing surface, with integrated pre-concentration zones that amplify signal for ultra-low concentrations.

3. **Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS)** — When PFAS molecules bind to the MIP cavities, they change the electrode's electrical impedance. A miniaturized potentiostat in the reader measures this change with high precision.

4. **Multi-zone architecture** — Separate electrode zones with PFOA-specific and PFOS-specific MIPs, plus a reference zone, enabling simultaneous detection and built-in quality control.

### Why MIPs over biological receptors
- **Thermal stability**: Works from 5°C to 45°C (no cold chain)
- **Chemical robustness**: Functions in real water matrices with varying pH, salinity, organics
- **Reproducibility**: Fabricated via electropolymerization — scalable, consistent
- **Shelf life**: 12+ months (vs. days/weeks for enzymes or antibodies)
- **Cost**: ~$0.50/sensor vs. $5-50 for biological recognition elements

### Fabrication
All processes use standard MEMS/cleanroom equipment:
- Glass substrate with Cr/Au electrode patterning (sputtering + lithography)
- MIP layer via electropolymerization
- PDMS or COC microfluidic channels via soft lithography
- Wafer-level bonding and dicing

## Competition

### Why now? The timing is perfect
1. **Regulatory**: EPA MCL at 4 ppt is law (April 2024). Extended deadline to 2031 gives time to build.
2. **Technology**: MIP sensors achieving sub-ppt detection only demonstrated in 2025
3. **Market**: No commercial product delivers quantitative, sub-4ppt, <15 min PFAS testing
4. **Infrastructure**: FREDsense validated market demand but product is too slow (3-4 hr)
5. **Science**: 2025-2026 publications prove the core sensing approach works

### Competitive moat
1. **Microsystems expertise**: MEMS chip design + fabrication is a genuine barrier
2. **MIP formulation IP**: Specific polymer/template combinations optimized for water matrices
3. **Integrated system design**: Chip + reader + cloud = lock-in
4. **Regulatory data**: Performance validation data accumulates over time
5. **Manufacturing scale**: Wafer-level fabrication = 1000s of chips per wafer
